About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out, and shared with others. Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith, and discover how Scripture can guide their everyday lives.

Through our online teaching sessions, Bible studies, prayer, discipleship, and spiritual discussions, we focus on building believers who are rooted in Christ and equipped to serve God and others.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to build, equip, encourage, and empower believers through the Word of God. We aim to create an environment where people can grow spiritually, develop Christian character, strengthen their prayer lives, and become more committed followers of Jesus Christ.

What We Teach

  • Biblical understanding and Christian foundations
  • Prayer and spiritual growth
  • Discipleship and following Christ
  • Faith and trusting God
  • Christian character and godly living
  • Forgiveness, love, and reconciliation
  • The work of the Holy Spirit
  • Discovering and using spiritual gifts
  • Applying God's Word in everyday life

Our Vision

Building the Church of Christ through biblical teaching, spiritual growth, discipleship, and practical Christian living.

Our Mission

To provide accessible biblical teaching that encourages believers to know God's Word, grow in faith, live according to biblical principles, and become effective disciples of Jesus Christ.
