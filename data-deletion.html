Biblical Empowerment Program · Data Deletion Instructions

B.E.P. Online respects your privacy. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your B.E.P. account or your interactions with our services.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bep.onlineprog@gmail.com with the subject Personal Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your full name and the email address or WhatsApp number associated with your B.E.P. account. If you use more than one account, identify the account you want deleted.
  3. Tell us whether you want your account closed and associated personal information deleted, or whether your request concerns specific information.
Please do not email passwords, identity document numbers, or other sensitive credentials. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account before acting on a request.

What happens next

We will review your request, verify it where necessary, and delete or de-identify personal information that we are not required or permitted to retain. We may retain limited records where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legitimate record-keeping obligations. If some information must be retained, we will explain this where appropriate.

We will contact you using the email address you provide about the outcome or if we need further information. Please allow a reasonable period for verification and processing.

WhatsApp and other service providers

If you have received B.E.P. communications through WhatsApp, include the relevant phone number in your request. Deleting information held by B.E.P. does not automatically delete information that Meta/WhatsApp or another service provider independently holds under its own policies. You may need to contact that provider separately.

Contact

For privacy and data deletion requests, contact bep.onlineprog@gmail.com.
