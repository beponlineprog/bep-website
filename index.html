About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out, and shared with others. Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith and encourage one another.

Through regular online teaching, discussions and Bible-based activities, B.E.P. seeks to equip believers to become stronger, more mature and more effective followers of Christ.

Our Vision

To see believers firmly established in God's Word, growing spiritually and becoming effective witnesses for Jesus Christ.

Rooted in the Word

Helping believers develop a strong biblical foundation and deeper understanding of Scripture.

Growing in Faith

Encouraging continuous spiritual growth, maturity and transformation through God's Word.

Building Believers

Creating an environment where believers can learn, encourage and strengthen one another.

Our Mission

Teach

To teach the Word of God faithfully and make biblical truth easier to understand and apply.

Equip

To equip believers with biblical knowledge and practical spiritual tools for everyday Christian living.

Transform

To encourage believers to allow God's Word to transform their character, choices and relationships.

What We Teach

Our teaching is centred on the Word of God and practical Christian living.

Bible Studies

Understanding Scripture, biblical principles and the message of God's Word.

Prayer

Developing a deeper prayer life and learning to depend on God.

Discipleship

Growing as followers of Christ and learning how to live out the Christian faith.

Spiritual Renewal

Understanding repentance, transformation and the importance of becoming renewed in Christ.
