About Us

Discover who we are, what we believe, and why B.E.P. exists.

Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.)

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out, and shared with others. Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith, and discover how God's Word can be applied to everyday life.

Our desire is to see believers become spiritually strengthened, biblically grounded, and equipped to live as faithful followers of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To see believers become firmly grounded in the Word of God, spiritually strengthened through biblical teaching, and equipped to live meaningful Christian lives that reflect Jesus Christ.

We desire to build believers who know God's Word, understand their faith, walk in obedience, and become a positive influence in their families, churches, communities, and workplaces.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible online biblical teaching that helps believers grow in their knowledge of Scripture, develop their spiritual lives, and become committed disciples of Jesus Christ.

  • Encourage deeper Bible study.
  • Strengthen prayer and spiritual growth.
  • Teach biblical principles.
  • Develop Christ-like character.
  • Encourage practical Christian living.
  • Equip believers to make disciples.
