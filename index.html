About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P) is a faith-driven initiative dedicated to equipping individuals with a deeper understanding of the Word of God and the transformative power of the Holy Spirit.

Our mission is to build spiritually grounded believers who are empowered to live purposeful, Christ-centered lives.

At B.E.P, we believe that true empowerment comes through knowledge, revelation, and application of Scripture.

Through structured teachings, interactive sessions, and Spirit-led discussions, we create a space where individuals can grow in faith, gain clarity in their spiritual journey, and develop a personal relationship with God.

Our program is designed for everyone—whether you are a new believer or someone seeking deeper spiritual insight.

We are committed to:

  • Teaching the Word of God with clarity and truth
  • Empowering believers through the Holy Spirit
  • Building a strong, united faith community
  • Encouraging spiritual growth and personal transformation

The Biblical Empowerment Program is more than just a teaching platform—it is a movement of believers rising in faith, purpose, and power.

Thursday Sessions

The Thursday sessions are held every week from 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM.

They are designed to deepen participants' understanding of the Word of God through structured teaching and interactive engagement.

The goal is to build a strong biblical foundation while encouraging spiritual growth and practical application of Scripture in daily life.

Each session encourages participants to ask questions, share comments, and participate in meaningful biblical discussions.

Objectives

  • Strengthening biblical understanding and spiritual maturity
  • Encouraging active participation through questions and discussion
  • Promoting clear and correct interpretation of Scripture
  • Building confidence in sharing and applying biblical knowledge
  • Fostering spiritual growth through collective learning

Our Focus

  • Discipleship
  • Prayer
  • Spiritual Growth
  • The Holy Spirit
  • Biblical Knowledge
  • Christian Character
