About B.E.P

Biblical Empowerment Program

B.E.P. is an online Christian teaching program focused on helping believers grow in their understanding of God's Word, develop their faith, strengthen their spiritual lives and become effective disciples of Jesus Christ.


Our vision: Building the Church of Christ through biblical teaching, spiritual growth and practical Christian living.

Our Sessions & Objectives

Study the Word

Understand Scripture and apply biblical principles to everyday life.

Grow Spiritually

Develop a stronger prayer life and deeper relationship with God.

Discipleship

Equip believers to follow Christ and help others grow in faith.

Christian Character

Learn to live with love, forgiveness, humility and integrity.
