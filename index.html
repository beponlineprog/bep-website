About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P) is a faith-driven initiative dedicated to equipping individuals with a deeper understanding of the Word of God and the transformative power of the Holy Spirit.

Our mission is to build spiritually grounded believers who are empowered to live purposeful, Christ-centered lives.

At B.E.P, we believe that true empowerment comes through knowledge, revelation, and application of Scripture.

We are committed to:

  • Teaching the Word of God with clarity and truth
  • Empowering believers through the Holy Spirit
  • Building a strong, united faith community
  • Encouraging spiritual growth and personal transformation

Thursday Sessions

The Thursday sessions are held every week from 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM.

They are designed to deepen participants' understanding of the Word of God through structured teaching and interactive engagement.

Each session encourages discussion, comments, questions, and practical application of Scripture.

Objectives

  • Strengthening biblical understanding
  • Encouraging active participation
  • Promoting correct interpretation of Scripture
  • Building confidence in sharing biblical knowledge
  • Fostering spiritual growth
