About Us

Growing believers through God's Word, discipleship and Christian fellowship.

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out and shared with others.

Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith and develop a stronger foundation in Scripture.

Through regular Bible teaching, discussions, prayer, discipleship and Bible activities, B.E.P. seeks to equip believers to live faithfully for Christ and serve others.

Our Vision

To see a spiritually mature, Christ-centred generation of believers who are firmly rooted in the Word of God, transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit, united in faith, and equipped to live out the teachings of Jesus Christ. We envision a Church that grows in truth, walks in love, serves with humility, and makes disciples who positively impact their families, communities, and the world for the glory of God.

Our Mission

To build and strengthen the Church of Christ through sound biblical teaching, intentional discipleship, prayer, fellowship, and spiritual development. We are committed to equipping believers with a deeper understanding of God's Word, nurturing a life of holiness and obedience, developing Christlike character, and preparing every member to serve faithfully in the body of Christ. Through the guidance of the Holy Spirit, we seek to raise committed disciples who put their faith into action, share the Gospel, encourage one another, and contribute to the growth and unity of the Church.

Our Guiding Scripture

Ephesians 4:12–13 (NIV)

“To equip his people for works of service, so that the body of Christ may be built up until we all reach unity in the faith and in the knowledge of the Son of God and become mature, attaining to the whole measure of the fullness of Christ.”
