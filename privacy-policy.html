B.E.P. Online — Privacy Policy

Biblical Empowerment Program · Building the Church of Christ

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Biblical Empowerment Program (“B.E.P.”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, register as a member or learner, participate in our programmes, or communicate with us, including through WhatsApp.

Our approach: We use personal information for programme administration, communication and the services you request. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use B.E.P. services, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, school or membership details, registration information, course activity, assessment responses and records of communications or consent. We may also receive technical information necessary to operate and secure the website and services.

2. How we use information

  • To create and administer member or learner accounts and verify registrations.
  • To provide Bible teaching, courses, learning materials, assessments and certificates.
  • To send programme notices, announcements and service messages through email or WhatsApp where appropriate and, for WhatsApp announcements, where you have opted in.
  • To respond to enquiries, provide support and maintain programme records.
  • To protect our services, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp communications

If you provide a WhatsApp number and choose to receive B.E.P. messages, we may use the WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta service providers to send programme announcements and related messages. WhatsApp may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive message delivery status information, such as sent, delivered, read or failed, to help administer communications. A read receipt may depend on the recipient’s WhatsApp settings and is not always available.

You can opt out of B.E.P. WhatsApp announcements at any time by contacting us and asking to withdraw your WhatsApp communication consent. We will stop sending those announcements, subject to any necessary processing to record and respect your request.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate our website and services, including hosting and infrastructure providers, database and authentication services, and communication providers such as Meta/WhatsApp. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or our services, or with your direction.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. Information may be stored or processed by service providers in countries outside South Africa. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including programme administration, account records, legal obligations, resolving disputes and enforcing our agreements. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, ask questions about how it is used, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires this. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

Where a learner is a child, a parent or legal guardian should provide or oversee registration and communication consent where required by applicable law. Please contact us if you believe a child's information has been provided to us inappropriately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact B.E.P.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or to withdraw WhatsApp communication consent, contact B.E.P. using the contact details published on our official website.
