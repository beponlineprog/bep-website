B.E.P. Bible Knowledge Quiz

Test your understanding of Scripture, biblical doctrine and Christian discipleship.

Quiz Instructions

  • There are 20 multiple-choice questions.
  • Each question carries 3 marks.
  • Total marks: 60.
  • Pass mark: 40/60.
  • All questions should be answered.
  • The quiz has a 30-minute time limit.
  • When the timer reaches zero, the quiz will be submitted automatically.
  • Unanswered questions at the end of the time limit will be marked incorrect.
TIME REMAINING
30:00
The quiz will automatically submit when time expires.

1. According to Romans 4, what is Paul's main point concerning Abraham?

2. In Exodus 19:5–6, Israel is described as what in God's covenant purpose?

3. Jesus' teaching in Mark 12:29–31 combines which two Old Testament commands?

4. What central theme is emphasized in Isaiah 53 concerning the Servant?

5. According to Jeremiah 31:31–34, what distinguishes the promised new covenant?

6. Hebrews 7 uses Melchizedek to explain what about Christ's priesthood?

7. In Daniel 7:13–14, what is given to the “Son of Man”?

8. In Matthew 5:17–20, how does Jesus describe His relationship to the Law and the Prophets?

9. What does Jesus' “vine and branches” teaching in John 15 emphasize?

10. According to Romans 6, what does union with Christ's death and resurrection signify for believers?

11. According to Romans 8:15–17, what does the Spirit enable believers to cry?

12. What does Paul's teaching in 1 Corinthians 12 imply about spiritual gifts?

13. What is Paul's argument in Galatians 2:16 regarding justification?

14. How should Ephesians 2:8–10 be understood as a whole?

15. What central example does Philippians 2:5–11 present?

16. What does Colossians 1:15–20 emphasize about Christ?

17. According to Hebrews 4:14–16, why can believers approach God's throne with confidence?

18. What point does James make in James 2 concerning faith?

19. According to 1 Peter 2:9, believers are described as what?

20. What is the central promise described in Revelation 21:1–4?
