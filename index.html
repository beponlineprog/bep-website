About Us

Growing believers through God's Word, discipleship and Christian fellowship.

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out and shared with others.

Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith and develop a stronger foundation in Scripture.

Through regular Bible teaching, discussions, prayer, discipleship and Bible activities, B.E.P. seeks to equip believers to live faithfully for Christ and serve others.

Our Vision

To see believers rooted in the Word of God, growing in faith, walking in spiritual maturity and becoming effective witnesses for Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To teach, equip and transform believers through biblical teaching, discipleship, prayer, fellowship and practical application of God's Word.

What We Teach

Our teaching focuses on helping believers understand Scripture and apply God's Word in everyday life.

Bible Studies

Understanding Scripture and discovering biblical principles for Christian living.

Prayer

Developing a deeper prayer life and dependence on God.

Discipleship

Growing as followers of Christ and helping others grow in faith.

Spiritual Renewal

Leaving the old life behind and pursuing spiritual transformation through Christ.
