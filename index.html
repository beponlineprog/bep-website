Biblical Empowerment Program

About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out, and shared with others.

Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith, strengthen their spiritual lives, and become effective disciples of Jesus Christ.

Our vision is to build the Church of Christ through the teaching of God's Word.

Thursday Sessions & Objectives

Biblical Teaching

Study God's Word and understand biblical principles that apply to everyday life.

Spiritual Growth

Develop a deeper relationship with God through prayer, faith and obedience.

Discipleship

Grow as disciples of Jesus Christ and learn how to share the Gospel.

Christian Character

Apply God's Word by developing Christ-like character and relationships.

Our Objectives

  • To teach and explain the Word of God.
  • To strengthen believers spiritually.
  • To encourage prayer and faith.
  • To develop effective disciples of Jesus Christ.
  • To help believers apply biblical principles in everyday life.
  • To build the Church of Christ through God's Word.
