About Us

The Biblical Empowerment Program (B.E.P.) is an online Christian teaching and discipleship program established to help believers grow deeper in their relationship with God and develop a stronger understanding of His Word.

At B.E.P., we believe that the Word of God is not only meant to be heard, but also understood, lived out, and shared with others.

Our program creates an opportunity for believers to learn biblical principles, ask questions, grow in faith, strengthen their spiritual lives, and become effective disciples of Jesus Christ.

Our vision is to build the Church of Christ through the teaching of God's Word.

Thursday Sessions & Objectives

Thursday Sessions

B.E.P. meets every Thursday from 19:00 to 20:30 through Microsoft Teams.

Each session focuses on biblical teaching, spiritual growth, discipleship, prayer, Christian character and practical application of God's Word.

Our Objectives

  • To deepen believers' understanding of Scripture.
  • To strengthen personal faith in God.
  • To encourage spiritual growth and maturity.
  • To develop committed disciples of Jesus Christ.
  • To teach believers how to apply God's Word.
  • To equip believers to share the Gospel.
  • To build and strengthen the Church of Christ.
